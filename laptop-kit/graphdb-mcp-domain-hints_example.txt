DOMAIN: Kaiser Permanente health-plan coverage knowledge graph (plans, benefits, cost sharing).

SCHEMA MAP (verified against the live repo):
- Plans: ?plan a onto:HealthPlanPolicy ; rdfs:label ?label .
  Plans also carry skos:altLabel with marketing names (e.g. 'Kaiser Permanente
  Senior Advantage Santa Clara Basic Plan (HMO)') and contract ids ('H0524-039') -
  match plan names against rdfs:label AND skos:altLabel.
  Medicare plans also carry onto:contractNumber (e.g. 'H0524') and
  onto:planBenefitPackageNumber (e.g. '039'); plan URIs look like data:plan/H0524-039.
- Documents: ?doc a onto:Document ; dcterms:title ?title ; onto:describes ?plan .
  Plans may also link prov:wasDerivedFrom to their source document node.
- Cost-share facts: ?plan onto:hasCoverageRule ?rule .
  ?rule a onto:CoverageRule | onto:CopayRule | onto:CoinsuranceRule |
         onto:DeductibleRule | onto:DrugTierRule ;
        onto:appliesToService ?svc ;
        onto:coverageStatus ?status ;      # 'covered' | 'conditional' | 'not-covered'
        onto:rawText ?raw .                # verbatim source wording - quote this in answers
  OPTIONAL: onto:copayAmount, onto:coveragePercentage, onto:networkTier,
            onto:coverageTier ('self-only' etc.), onto:deductibleAmount,
            onto:outOfPocketMaximum (on DeductibleRule, for Individual & Family plans),
            onto:requiresPriorAuthorization (boolean), onto:countsTowardMoop (boolean).
  Services: ?svc a onto:HealthCareService ; rdfs:label ?svcLabel .

RECIPE - ALWAYS DO THIS FIRST:
1. Resolve the member's plan to a plan URI before anything else. Match tokens from the
   plan name with CONTAINS(LCASE(...)) on plan rdfs:label AND on document dcterms:title
   (then follow onto:describes). Example tokens: 'senior advantage', 'santa clara',
   'basic'. Never require an exact label match.
2. Scope every subsequent query to that plan URI via onto:hasCoverageRule.
3. For benefit questions, filter service labels with CONTAINS(LCASE(?svcLabel), ...)
   on a distinctive word ('mental', 'therapy', 'ambulance', ...).

WORKED EXAMPLE (mental-health cost sharing for one plan; returns copays + verbatim text):
SELECT DISTINCT ?svcLabel ?ruleType ?copay ?status ?raw WHERE {
  ?plan a onto:HealthPlanPolicy ; rdfs:label ?planLabel ; onto:hasCoverageRule ?rule .
  FILTER(CONTAINS(LCASE(STR(?planLabel)), 'santa clara') && CONTAINS(LCASE(STR(?planLabel)), 'basic'))
  ?rule onto:appliesToService ?svc ; a ?ruleType ; onto:coverageStatus ?status ; onto:rawText ?raw .
  OPTIONAL { ?rule onto:copayAmount ?copay }
  ?svc rdfs:label ?svcLabel .
  FILTER(CONTAINS(LCASE(STR(?svcLabel)), 'mental') || CONTAINS(LCASE(STR(?svcLabel)), 'therapy'))
  FILTER(STRSTARTS(STR(?ruleType), STR(onto:)))
}

GOTCHAS:
- Service labels may contain stray bullet glyphs and duplicate variants; always match
  with CONTAINS(LCASE(...)), never equality, and DISTINCT your results.
- RDFS entailment adds rdfs:Resource / prov:Entity rows to 'a ?type' queries; keep
  FILTER(STRSTARTS(STR(?ruleType), STR(onto:))) when selecting rule types.
- coverageStatus 'not-covered' is a definitive negative - answer 'no' confidently and
  cite rawText.
- Prefer these typed facts over document chunks for any dollar amount, copay,
  coinsurance, deductible, or out-of-pocket figure; include rawText as the citation.
- Prior authorization and out-of-pocket-maximum treatment come ONLY from the typed
  facts onto:requiresPriorAuthorization and onto:countsTowardMoop on the service's own
  rule. Never infer them from dagger/asterisk footnote legends seen in chunk text -
  those legends repeat on every page and apply only to explicitly marked services.
  If the typed fact is absent, do not claim prior auth is required or that costs are
  excluded from the maximum out-of-pocket.
- Inpatient psychiatric care and partial hospitalization are DIFFERENT services with
  different cost sharing; never present one's amounts as the other's.
